AION Personal Research Intake

To request deletion, email felipeacha@gmail.com with the subject AION Personal Research Intake – Data Deletion Request.

What to include

  • Your Instagram username.
  • Enough detail to identify the relevant interaction or research item.
  • A contact address for the response.

Do not send your password, access token, or other authentication secrets.

What happens next

The request will be acknowledged and limited verification may be requested to protect against unauthorized deletion. Personal data under the controller's control will then be deleted or de-identified, except information that must be retained temporarily for security, legal obligations, dispute prevention, or constrained backups. Confirmation will be sent when processing is complete.
